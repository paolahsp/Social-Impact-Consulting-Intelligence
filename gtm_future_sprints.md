# Intellectus Future GTM Sprints

These post-MVP sprints translate the current strategy hypotheses into
observable market tests. The initial beachhead is European boutique
social-impact consultancies. The buyer hypothesis is a Founder, Managing
Director, or Practice Lead; the primary user hypothesis is a Senior Consultant
or Manager.

## Sprint 1 — Validate the live workflow moment

- **Goal:** Determine whether a Public Quick Scan improves a real
  pre-engagement decision, such as preparing a discovery call, workshop, or
  proposal.
- **Target user/buyer:** Senior Consultant or Manager as user; Founder,
  Managing Director, or Practice Lead as sponsor.
- **Channel or motion:** Founder-led outreach through trusted introductions,
  relevant professional communities, and targeted conversations with
  social-impact consulting practices.
- **Key deliverable:** One consultant-reviewed Public Quick Scan for a real
  organization, plus a review log showing what the consultant kept, corrected,
  excluded, or converted into a client question.
- **Success metric:** The output is used in a real client-work moment and the
  review log provides observable evidence of incremental usefulness. If the
  consultant must recreate the work from scratch, the sprint does not pass.

## Sprint 2 — Test repeat use and human-review fit

- **Goal:** Learn whether the workflow is useful enough to be used again and
  whether human review is substantive without eliminating the product's value.
- **Target user/buyer:** The same consulting user and, where relevant, their
  methodology or practice lead.
- **Channel or motion:** Concierge design-partner pilot followed by continued
  access and observation; the team does not schedule the second use for the
  participant.
- **Key deliverable:** A second case initiated by the consulting practice, a
  documented human-review procedure, and a comparison of edits and reuse across
  both cases.
- **Success metric:** A second case is initiated because the practice chooses
  to use the workflow, with reviewer decisions and overrides recorded. Positive
  feedback without repeat behavior does not count as success.

## Sprint 3 — Validate the buyer and commercial path

- **Goal:** Identify the economic buyer, approval path, and unit of value for a
  bounded paid pilot.
- **Target user/buyer:** Founder, Managing Director, or Practice Lead, with
  methodology, privacy/security, and procurement stakeholders included when
  relevant.
- **Channel or motion:** Founder-led paid-pilot proposal to practices that
  completed the workflow and repeat-use tests.
- **Key deliverable:** A bounded paid-pilot package, named decision chain,
  documented objections, and a buying-path map from sponsor to approval.
- **Success metric:** A real paid commitment or explicit budget trade-off from
  a named buyer. If no commitment is made, the sprint still produces a clear
  no-go reason and a decision to change the segment, package, or buying motion.

## Guardrails across all sprints

- Use real workflow behavior, not generic expressions of interest, as evidence.
- Keep consultant review visible and recorded.
- Do not claim validated pricing, ROI, time savings, or client outcomes before
  evidence exists.
- Request internal or connected client data only after value, authorization,
  privacy, and security requirements are established.
